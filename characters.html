Main Characters

Seron Darkness

Once the legendary leader of The Guardians, Seron Darkness stepped away from active combat after the Final War, choosing instead to guide the galaxy from behind the scenes. Though retired, Seron remains one of the most influential figures in the Zavila Galaxy, coordinating missions, monitoring threats, and advising commanders through encrypted long‑range communication networks.

Seron’s presence is felt everywhere — his strategies, philosophies, and battle doctrines still shape the Unified Guardians. Many younger recruits view him as a mythic figure, while veterans know him as the quiet architect of peace. Despite his retirement, Seron continues to carry the weight of the galaxy, preparing for the day when ancient forces rise again.

Kole Rogue

A former frontline Guardian known for precision, discipline, and unmatched tactical awareness, Kole Rogue transitioned into a full‑time Guardian trainer after the war. His training programs are considered the gold standard across the Unified Guardians, producing elite soldiers capable of defending entire star systems.

Kole’s leadership style is strict but fair — he pushes recruits to their limits while teaching them the values that defined the original Guardians: unity, resilience, and honor. Though retired from combat, Kole remains battle‑ready, and many believe he would return to the field without hesitation if Seron ever called upon him.

Michael Rogue

After years of service as a Guardian, Michael Rogue chose a peaceful civilian life following the Final War. He settled into a quiet existence, far from the chaos of interstellar conflict, becoming a symbol of what peace truly means for former warriors.

Though no longer active, Michael’s legacy remains strong. His past missions, acts of bravery, and loyalty to The Guardians are still remembered by those who fought beside him. Many young Guardians seek him out for wisdom, believing that his perspective — shaped by both war and peace — offers guidance no battlefield commander can provide.

Andrew OverDark

Andrew OverDark retired to his homeworld, OverDarkis, a planet known for its quiet landscapes and deep cultural traditions. Once a fierce Guardian with unmatched endurance, Andrew now lives a calm life, reconnecting with his roots and the people he once fought to protect.

Despite his peaceful lifestyle, Andrew remains spiritually connected to the Unified Guardians. He is often consulted during times of uncertainty, offering wisdom shaped by years of conflict and personal reflection. Many believe that Andrew’s understanding of balance — between war and peace — will become vital as new threats emerge across the Zavila Galaxy.

HawkBat

Known for his speed, sharp instincts, and unmatched aerial combat skills, HawkBat retired from frontline duty and now serves as a Guardian trainer in Zavara. His training focuses on mobility, strategy, and rapid‑response tactics, shaping recruits into agile defenders capable of reacting to threats anywhere in the galaxy.

HawkBat’s reputation is legendary — many Guardians credit him for saving entire squads during the Final War. Though retired, he remains deeply committed to the Unified Guardians, believing that the next generation must be prepared for dangers far greater than anything the galaxy has faced before.
