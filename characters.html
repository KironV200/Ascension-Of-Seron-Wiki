Main Characters

Seron Darkness

Once the legendary leader of The Guardians, Seron Darkness stepped away from active combat after the Final War, choosing instead to guide the galaxy from behind the scenes. Though retired, Seron remains one of the most influential figures in the Zavila Galaxy, coordinating missions, monitoring threats, and advising commanders through encrypted long‑range communication networks.

Seron’s presence is felt everywhere — his strategies, philosophies, and battle doctrines still shape the Unified Guardians. Many younger recruits view him as a mythic figure, while veterans know him as the quiet architect of peace. Despite his retirement, Seron continues to carry the weight of the galaxy, preparing for the day when ancient forces rise again.

Kole Rogue

A former frontline Guardian known for precision, discipline, and unmatched tactical awareness, Kole Rogue transitioned into a full‑time Guardian trainer after the war. His training programs are considered the gold standard across the Unified Guardians, producing elite soldiers capable of defending entire star systems.

Kole’s leadership style is strict but fair — he pushes recruits to their limits while teaching them the values that defined the original Guardians: unity, resilience, and honor. Though retired from combat, Kole remains battle‑ready, and many believe he would return to the field without hesitation if Seron ever called upon him.

Michael Rogue

After years of service as a Guardian, Michael Rogue chose a peaceful civilian life following the Final War. He settled into a quiet existence, far from the chaos of interstellar conflict, becoming a symbol of what peace truly means for former warriors.

Though no longer active, Michael’s legacy remains strong. His past missions, acts of bravery, and loyalty to The Guardians are still remembered by those who fought beside him. Many young Guardians seek him out for wisdom, believing that his perspective — shaped by both war and peace — offers guidance no battlefield commander can provide.

Andrew OverDark

Andrew OverDark retired to his homeworld, OverDarkis, a planet known for its quiet landscapes and deep cultural traditions. Once a fierce Guardian with unmatched endurance, Andrew now lives a calm life, reconnecting with his roots and the people he once fought to protect.

Despite his peaceful lifestyle, Andrew remains spiritually connected to the Unified Guardians. He is often consulted during times of uncertainty, offering wisdom shaped by years of conflict and personal reflection. Many believe that Andrew’s understanding of balance — between war and peace — will become vital as new threats emerge across the Zavila Galaxy.

HawkBat Rogue

Known for his speed, sharp instincts, and unmatched aerial combat skills, HawkBat retired from frontline duty and now serves as a Guardian trainer in Zavara. His training focuses on mobility, strategy, and rapid‑response tactics, shaping recruits into agile defenders capable of reacting to threats anywhere in the galaxy.

HawkBat’s reputation is legendary — many Guardians credit him for saving entire squads during the Final War. Though retired, he remains deeply committed to the Unified Guardians, believing that the next generation must be prepared for dangers far greater than anything the galaxy has faced before.

Tom Letanio

Tom Letanio, once a respected Guardian, now rules the realm of Hell as the enigmatic King Indigo. Despite his fearsome title, Tom maintains a complex relationship with the Unified Guardians, offering assistance only when situations demand his unique influence or power. His leadership in Hell is defined by strict order, balance, and an unwavering sense of justice that keeps the chaotic realm stable.

Though distant from the galaxy’s politics, Tom’s connection to the Guardians remains strong. His insight into supernatural threats and ancient forces makes him a valuable ally when the galaxy faces dangers beyond mortal understanding.

Dallas Red-Star

Dallas Red-Star dedicated his post-war life to preserving the history of the Guardians. As one of the most respected historians in the galaxy, Dallas documents battles, heroes, fallen comrades, and the evolution of the Unified Guardians. His archives serve as the foundation for future generations seeking to understand the past.

Dallas’s work ensures that the sacrifices of countless Guardians are never forgotten. His research often uncovers forgotten threats and ancient mysteries that still influence the galaxy today.

Nathan Ice

Nathan Ice, known for his calm precision and icy resolve, now leads the IceBerg Tower — a massive fortress and research hub dedicated to cryotechnology, planetary defense, and environmental stabilization. His leadership transformed the tower into one of the galaxy’s most advanced scientific centers.

Nathan remains deeply loyal to the Guardians, offering technological support and strategic insight whenever threats emerge that require cold logic and colder firepower.

Connor Violet

Connor Violet oversees the Super Soldier Program, a cutting-edge initiative designed to enhance physical and mental capabilities of elite defenders across the galaxy. His work blends science, discipline, and innovation, pushing the boundaries of what a Guardian can become.

Though retired from active duty, Connor’s influence is felt through every enhanced soldier trained under his program. His dedication ensures the galaxy remains protected by the strongest warriors ever created.

David Spike

David Spike retired to Guardia, choosing a quiet civilian life after years of service. Known for his reliability and steady presence during the war, David now enjoys peace, community, and the simple joys of everyday life.

Despite stepping away from conflict, David remains a beloved figure among locals, often sharing stories of his Guardian days with younger generations who admire his humility and courage.

Angelo Pierce

Angelo Pierce transitioned from Guardian service to become a skilled engineer and mechanic for a major starship manufacturing corporation. His expertise in propulsion systems, armor plating, and starship design makes him one of the most valuable engineers in the industry.

Angelo’s innovations have improved countless vessels across the galaxy. Though retired, he still answers the call when the Guardians require specialized engineering for high-risk missions.

Esteban Maverick

Esteban Maverick left the Guardians to walk his own path as a vigilante gunslinger. Operating outside official channels, Esteban protects frontier worlds, remote colonies, and lawless regions where the Unified Guardians cannot always intervene.

His reputation as a lone defender has spread across the galaxy. Though unpredictable, Esteban’s moral compass remains strong, and he often appears when people need him most.

Royal Demetrios

Royal Demetrios, like Dallas Red-Star, dedicated his retirement to documenting Guardian history. His focus lies in cultural evolution, faction development, and the personal stories of Guardians who shaped the galaxy’s fate.

Royal’s archives complement Dallas’s work, creating a complete historical record that preserves both the grand battles and the intimate human stories behind them.

Peter Hydros

Peter Hydros embraced a quiet life as a lumberjack in Zavara’s dense forests. Once a Guardian known for strength and endurance, Peter now channels his abilities into sustainable forestry and environmental protection.

His peaceful lifestyle reflects the galaxy’s new era of stability, though Peter remains ready to act if danger ever threatens Zavara or the Guardians he once fought beside.

Samuel Andreas

Samuel Andreas took an unexpected path after retiring from the Guardians — becoming a standup comedian. His humor, shaped by years of hardship and camaraderie, resonates across the galaxy, bringing laughter to civilians and veterans alike.

Samuel’s performances often highlight the absurdity of war, the quirks of Guardian life, and the hope found in unity. His comedy has become a symbol of healing in the post-war era.
